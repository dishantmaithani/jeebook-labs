SKYWARD

A flight-physics simulator. Fly a small single-engine plane over an island, and watch the four forces that keep it in the air.

▲ LiftMade by the wing as air flows over it. Grows with speed² and angle of attack.
▼ Weightmg = 1100 kg × 9.8 ≈ 10.8 kN, always straight down.
► ThrustThe propeller pulls the plane forward. You control it with the throttle.
◄ DragAir resistance pointing against your motion. More lift also means more drag.
L = ½ ρ v² S CL  ·  double your speed and you get 4× the lift. Pull the stick back to raise the angle of attack α and CL rises with it, up to about 15°. Past that, the air separates from the wing and lift collapses. That is a stall.
