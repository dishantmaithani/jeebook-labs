PRIMORDIAL

You're a single cell in an ancient sea. Eat to gain energy and 🧬 DNA, then spend DNA on mutations so you survive.

Every other creature evolves on its own. When they reproduce, their children mutate slightly. The ones that are too slow, too hungry, or too easy to catch die out. Watch the chart in the corner and you'll see natural selection happen live.

● Green = plant-eaters   ● Red = predators. A predator can eat anything noticeably smaller than itself.
