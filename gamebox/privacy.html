Game Box – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Game Box is a collection of small offline games.

Data we collect

None. Game Box does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your best scores are saved only on your own device so they are there next time you play. They never leave your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Permissions

Game Box only uses the vibration permission (to buzz on game events). It does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the app is safe for players of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
